# Meta Team Templates — Champions Reg M-A

Reference these when building teams. Each template is a battle-tested core.
Users can swap 1-2 Pokemon but the CORE should remain intact.

---

## 🌧️ Rain Team (A-tier)

**Core:**
- Pelipper (Drizzle, Focus Sash) — Rain setter + Tailwind
- Archaludon (Stamina, Assault Vest) — Special attacker with Electro Shot (100% accurate in rain)
- Basculegion (Swift Swim, Choice Band) — Fast physical attacker, Wave Crash OHKOs in rain
- Dragonite or Mega Dragonite (Multiscale / Inner Focus, Dragoninite) — Coverage + Multiscale bulk

**Flex slots:** Incineroar (Intimidate pivot), Sinistcha (Rage Powder support)

**Win condition:** Get rain up, boost Archaludon's Stamina, spam Electro Shot + CB Wave Crash.

**Why Basculegion gets Swift Swim, not Adaptability:** on a rain team, Swift Swim doubles speed giving you speed control. Adaptability only buffs damage without speed — redundant with Choice Band.

**Why 1 rain setter, not 2:** Pelipper is enough. The second slot should be a rain ABUSER (Basculegion) not another setter.

## ☀️ Sun Team (A-tier)

**Core:**
- Charizard-Mega-Y (Drought, Charizardite Y) — Sun setter + nuke
- Venusaur (Chlorophyll, Life Orb) — Fast Grass sweeper in sun
- Whimsicott (Prankster, Covert Cloak) — Tailwind / redirect support
- Incineroar (Intimidate) — Glue

**Flex:** Torkoal (alt Drought setter), Meowscarada (Chlorophyll backup)

**Win condition:** Sun up, Chlorophyll abusers clean.

---

## 🏜️ Sand Team (S-tier threat — scary for rain)

**Core:**
- Tyranitar (Sand Stream, Assault Vest) — Sand setter + 1.5x SpD in sand
- Excadrill (Sand Rush, Life Orb / Choice Scarf) — Doubles speed in sand → 280+ speed
- Garchomp (Rough Skin, Life Orb) — Ground coverage + Rock Slide

**Flex:** Incineroar, Sinistcha, Kingambit

**Win condition:** Excadrill wins speed war in sand, KOs everything with Earthquake + Iron Head.

**Key threat to rain teams:** Sand overrides rain on Tyranitar switch-in. Rain teams lose Swift Swim, Hurricane accuracy, rain-boosted Water moves.

## 🔮 Trick Room Team (A-tier)

**Core:**
- Farigiraf (Armor Tail, Weakness Policy) — TR setter, can't be Fake Out'd
- Sinistcha (Hospitality, Sitrus Berry) — Backup TR + heal
- Dondozo (Unaware) — Bulky tank
- Incineroar (Intimidate)

**Flex:** Kingambit (Sucker Punch ignores speed), slow attackers

**Win condition:** Set TR, slow attackers sweep.

## ⚔️ Hyper Offense (S-tier)

**Core:**
- Dragapult (Clear Body, Focus Sash) — Fast Ghost/Dragon sweeper
- Sneasler (Unburden, Focus Sash) — Post-Sash becomes fastest thing alive
- Metagross-Mega (Tough Claws, Metagrossite) — Physical powerhouse
- Kingambit (Defiant, Black Glasses) — Sucker Punch priority

**Flex:** Incineroar, Whimsicott for Tailwind support

**Win condition:** Overwhelm before opponent can set up. Tailwind + priority moves + 4x weakness exploitation.

---

## 🌀 Perish Trap (Niche but deadly)

**Core:**
- Gengar-Mega (Shadow Tag, Gengarite) — Traps opponents
- Politoed (Drizzle, Damp Rock) — Drizzle + Perish Song
- Whimsicott (Prankster) — Tailwind / redirect

**Win condition:** Trap opponent with Shadow Tag, Perish Song counts down, they can't switch out.

**Counter:** Sinistcha (Ghost-type = immune to Shadow Tag).

## S-Tier Meta Staples (use these in MOST teams)

These Pokemon appear on 30%+ of teams for a reason — they're just that good:

1. **Incineroar** — Intimidate + Fake Out. Fits every team.
2. **Archaludon** — Rain's best friend. Even without rain, Assault Vest Stamina is great.
3. **Sneasler** — Steel-killer. OHKOs Archaludon mirror, Kingambit, Tyranitar.
4. **Sinistcha** — Rage Powder + Hospitality + Ghost immunity to Shadow Tag.
5. **Incineroar** again — seriously, just put it on the team.

If you're NOT using any of these, you need a strong reason.

---

## When to Deviate from Top Meta

The agent can suggest non-top-meta Pokemon when:
- User specifically asks for a niche archetype
- User's playstyle strongly prefers a specific mon
- A clear synergy exists (e.g., Dragonite on rain team for Multiscale + Ground immunity)
- Surprise factor matters (unexpected lead catches opponents off guard)

Otherwise, default to S-tier staples.
