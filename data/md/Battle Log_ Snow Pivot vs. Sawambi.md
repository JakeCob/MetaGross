# Battle Log: Snow Pivot vs. Sawambi (Hyper Offense)

## 🏆 Post-Match Analysis

### 🔍 The Opponent's Strategy: "Fake & Nuke"

The opponent (Sawambi) ran an aggressive Hyper Offense core. This team is designed to force the opponent into a corner on Turn 1 and sweep before they can set up their defensive game plan.

* **Mega Lopunny (The Disruptor):** Base 135 Speed. Designed to move first, click Fake Out, and stop the opponent from setting up defenses or speed control.  
* **Hisuian Typhlosion (The Nuke):** Paired with Lopunny, it aims to fire off a full-health, spread-damage **Eruption** while the opponent is flinched.  
* **Clefable (The Shield):** Uses **Follow Me** to draw single-target attacks away from their frail sweepers, ensuring they survive to deal damage.  
* **Araquanid (The Bulky Nuke):** Its *Water Bubble* ability doubles the power of Water moves, prevents burns, and halves Fire damage. It served as a specific counter to Fire-type checks like Incineroar.

---

### 🧠 Key Turning Points & Tactics

**1\. The Speed Flip (Turn 3\)** When Lopunny Mega Evolved, it dictated the pace. However, after predicting a Protect, Milotic landed **Icy Wind** on an exposed Lopunny, dropping its Speed to \-1. This instantly made Garchomp faster, ripping the speed advantage away and dismantling their offensive pressure.

**2\. The Garchomp "Meat Grinder"** Garchomp was used effectively as a massive liability for the opponent through positioning and its ability:

* **Rough Skin Value:** Araquanid used contact moves (Liquidation and Lunge) on Garchomp multiple times. *Rough Skin* passively chipped Araquanid down to 13% HP without Garchomp needing to aim a single direct attack at it.  
* **Bypassing Redirection:** When Clefable entered to use *Follow Me*, Garchomp bypassed the redirection entirely by spamming **Rock Slide**, a spread move. This damaged Clefable heavily while continuing to hit the Araquanid hiding behind it.

**3\. The Checkmate Pivot (Turn 9\)** When Clefable fainted, the opponent's Mega Lopunny became active again, threatening a Fake Out. Swapping the exhausted Garchomp for **Incineroar** secured the final checkmate:

* The switch triggered **Intimidate** to drop Mega Lopunny's Attack.  
* Milotic followed up with **Scald**, landing a crucial Burn on Lopunny.  
* Between Intimidate and the Burn, Mega Lopunny's physical damage was effectively reduced by 75%, creating a mathematical wall they could not punch through.

---

### 📈 Final Team Takeaways

This battle demonstrated the immense value of **Milotic** as a bulky support anchor. It survived the entire game, dropped opposing speeds with Icy Wind, and secured the game-winning Scald burn. The defensive core of Incineroar and Milotic successfully smothered and out-positioned a highly aggressive hyper-offensive team through superior board control.
