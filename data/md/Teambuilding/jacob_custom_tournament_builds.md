## 🐍 Team 4: Zoroark Deception — FINALIZED

*Updated from my earlier draft with damage-calc-informed EV refinements.*

### The Full Build

**1. Hisuian Zoroark @ Focus Sash** *(disguised as Kingambit)*
- Ability: Illusion
- Nature: Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: **Ghost** *(doubles Bitter Malice STAB for OHKO on Sinistcha/Mega Delphox)*
- Moves: **Bitter Malice / Hyper Voice / Icy Wind / Protect**

**2. Aerodactyl @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Unnerve
- Nature: Jolly
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Flying
- Moves: **Tailwind / Rock Slide / Wide Guard / Protect**

**3. Mega Floette @ Floettite**
- Ability: Flower Veil → Fairy Aura (post-Mega)
- Nature: Modest
- EVs: **252 HP / 188 SpA / 4 SpD / 64 Spe** (IVs: 0 Atk)
- 64 Spe creeps max-speed Jolly Garchomp under Tailwind
- Tera Type: **Poison** *(resists Fighting 0.5x — critical vs. Sneasler Close Combat)*
- Moves: **Light of Ruin / Calm Mind / Moonblast / Protect**

**4. Incineroar @ Safety Goggles**
- Ability: Intimidate
- Nature: Careful
- EVs: 244 HP / 4 Atk / 4 Def / 252 SpD / 4 Spe
- Tera Type: Grass (anti-Fighting, anti-Water)
- Moves: **Fake Out / Parting Shot / Flare Blitz / Knock Off**

**5. Sneasler @ White Herb**
- Ability: Unburden
- Nature: Jolly
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Fighting
- Moves: **Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / Protect**

**6. Kingambit @ Assault Vest** *(slot 6 — Zoroark's illusion target)*
- Ability: Supreme Overlord
- Nature: Adamant
- EVs: 220 HP / 252 Atk / 36 Spe
- Tera Type: **Flying** *(anti-Fighting/Ground)*
- Moves: **Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave / Iron Head / Low Kick**

### The Illusion Mechanics

**Rules for Zoroark to stay hidden:**
1. Kingambit MUST be in slot 6. Zoroark copies the last non-fainted teammate.
2. Once Zoroark takes ANY damage (even Hail, Sandstorm), illusion breaks.
3. If Kingambit faints before Zoroark enters battle, Zoroark will copy the next-last teammate.
4. Team preview shows the "disguise" Pokémon — opponents see Kingambit twice in memory, one real and one Zoroark.

### Primary Game Plan

**Lead:** "Kingambit" (Zoroark) + Aerodactyl
- Opponent sees two physical threats on your side
- They likely Fake Out "Kingambit" (common answer to Sucker Punch threat) → Zoroark Ghost-type = **Fake Out is immune**
- They might click Fighting coverage on "Kingambit" → immune
- You get a free turn: Aerodactyl Tailwind, Zoroark Icy Wind or Bitter Malice

**Turn 2 pivot:** Bring Mega Floette under Tailwind. Calm Mind + Light of Ruin sweeps.

**Win condition:** Mega Floette +1/+2 Light of Ruin cleans the field.

### Updated Damage Thresholds (post-EV tuning)

- Mega Floette at 188 SpA still OHKOs Sneasler, Garchomp, Basculegion
- Mega Floette 252 HP + Tera Poison **survives** Sneasler Close Combat (40–50% damage)
- 64 Spe Floette outspeeds all Jolly Garchomp under Tailwind — critical

- Sneasler/Kingambit for Goodstuffs mirrors
- Mega Floette for Dragon/Dark-heavy teams
- Aerodactyl Tailwind for speed control
- Rotom-Wash for fire types and physical walls

## 🧊 Team 8: Jacob's TR Cold Core *(inspired by Dray's 7-0 sweep)*

**Source:** Dray, 1st place at 30-player Northern Ontario VGC Tour (April 15, 2026) — 7-0 record. Original team: Froslass / Palafin / Kingambit / Hisuian Arcanine / Sinistcha / Sneasler.

**Jacob's tweak:** You already have **Mega Froslass** tuned from your Snow v2 team. This team keeps Dray's core but swaps Palafin → Mega Kangaskhan. Palafin is a great wincon but requires switch-in to activate Hero form; Mega Kangaskhan is immediately online with Parental Bond, reducing your risk.

### The Team

**1. Mega Froslass @ Froslassite**
- Ability: Snow Warning (post-Mega: Moody or other — check Champions data; Snow Warning confirmed)
- Nature: Timid
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Ghost
- Moves: **Blizzard / Shadow Ball / Icy Wind / Protect**

*Snow Warning sets weather that boosts Blizzard accuracy to 100% AND activates Slush Rush/Snow Cloak. Reuse your existing Mega Froslass spread.*

- EVs: 84 HP / 252 Atk / 4 Def / 4 SpD / 164 Spe

- Moves: **Double-Edge / Sucker Punch / Low Kick / Protect**

*Parental Bond Double-Edge under TR = game-ending damage. Not as explosive as Palafin Hero form but much more reliable.*

**Note:** You can only have ONE Mega per team. Since Mega Kangaskhan is the wincon here, Mega Froslass won't Mega in this team. **Drop the Mega stone on Froslass**, use regular Froslass with Snow Warning or Cursed Body ability.

- Win con: Speed + Intimidate tempo until you can bring Kangaskhan for Sucker Punch.

### Why This Team Wins

Dual-mode: you can play Trick Room OR speed offense, based on the matchup. Most opponents scout you as "TR" from team preview and bring anti-TR; you surprise them with Sneasler speed mode.

## 💧 Team 9: Jacob's Milotic Balance *(inspired by Navizz0's 3rd place)*

**Source:** Navizz0 (GB), 3rd place at 87-player Volticons Liberation Crusade #1 — 8-2 record. Original team: Delphox / Milotic / Clefable / Sneasler / Kingambit / Dragonite.

**Jacob's tweak:** Upgrade Delphox → Mega Delphox. Delphox gets a Mega in Champions with high Speed + SpA. Original Navizz0 team likely used base Delphox; Mega makes it a real wincon.

### The Team

**1. Mega Delphox @ Delphoxite**
- Ability: Magician → Blaze (post-Mega, or check for new ability)
- Nature: Timid
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Grass *(anti-Ground for opposing EQ spread)*
- Moves: **Flamethrower / Dazzling Gleam / Mystical Fire / Protect**

*Mystical Fire lowers target's SpA by -1 on hit — status utility while attacking.*

**2. Milotic @ Leftovers**
- Ability: **Competitive**
- Nature: Bold (+Def, -Atk)
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpA (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Water
- Moves: **Coil / Hypnosis / Scald / Recover**

*The Coil+Hypnosis core. Post-1-Coil: Hypnosis at 78% accuracy. Competitive gives +2 SpA if Intimidated — huge vs. opposing Incineroar.*

**3. Clefable @ Life Orb**
- Ability: **Magic Guard**
- Nature: Modest
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Steel
- Moves: **Moonblast / Follow Me / Helping Hand / Protect**

*Magic Guard = no Life Orb recoil, no burn damage, no hazard damage. Free Follow Me redirection for Milotic setup turns.*

**4. Sneasler @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Unburden
- Nature: Jolly
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Fighting
- Moves: **Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / Protect**

**5. Kingambit @ Black Glasses**
- Ability: Defiant
- Nature: Adamant
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Flying
- Moves: **Kowtow Cleave / Sucker Punch / Swords Dance / Protect**

**6. Dragonite @ Lum Berry**
- Ability: Multiscale
- Nature: Adamant
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Normal
- Moves: **Dragon Dance / Extreme Speed / Earthquake / Protect**

### Game Plan

**Primary Lead:** Clefable + Milotic
- Turn 1: Clefable Follow Me, Milotic Coil (+1 Atk/Def/Acc).
- Turn 2: Clefable Follow Me again, Milotic Hypnosis (78%) on biggest threat.
- Turn 3: Opponent is sleeping. Milotic Coil again or Scald the remaining mon.

**Aggro Lead:** Sneasler + Kingambit
- Turn 1: Fake Out + Kowtow spread damage.

**Backup Wincon:** Dragon Dance Dragonite or Clefable Life Orb Moonblast.

### Why This Team Wins

**Magic Guard Clefable + Coil Milotic is a passive engine.** You're not trying to win turn 1; you're grinding your opponent down turn-by-turn with sleep, burn (Scald), and screens-less bulky offense. Perfect for Bo3 where patience wins.
