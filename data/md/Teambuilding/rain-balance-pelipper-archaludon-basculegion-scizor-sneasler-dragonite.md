# Rain Balance with Dual Mega Options

_MetaGross Agent · 2026-04-26_

**Archetype:** Rain Balance

## Overview

A rain balance team built around Pelipper enabling Archaludon, Basculegion, and special Mega Dragonite while Mega Scizor supplies the anti-Fairy mode. The team should not always lead Pelipper; in weather wars, Pelipper is often a midgame weather pivot.

## Game Plan

Default to Sneasler + Archaludon or Sneasler + Dragonite leads, with Pelipper pivoting in after opposing weather or speed control commits. Choose Mega Scizor into Fairy-heavy games and Mega Dragonite into Venusaur/Farigiraf/Blastoise or sun-adjacent games; it is acceptable to bring no Mega into sand.

## Win Conditions
- Midgame rain swing with Pelipper enabling Archaludon Electro Shot and Basculegion Wave Crash.
- Mega Scizor priority cleanup into Fairy-heavy or chipped fast teams.
- Mega Dragonite special pressure under rain with Hurricane into Venusaur, Sneasler, Whimsicott, and neutral balance.

## Matchups

### vs Sand
**Leads**: Lead Sneasler + Archaludon | Back Pelipper + Basculegion

Do not lead Pelipper into Tyranitar weather. Pressure Tyranitar with Close Combat, stabilize with Archaludon, then pivot Pelipper in after Tyranitar is weakened or forced defensive.

### vs Sun
**Leads**: Lead Sneasler + Dragonite | Back Pelipper + Archaludon

Let Charizard commit Mega weather before Pelipper enters. Use Fake Out plus Dragonite pressure to blunt the first sun turn, then reclaim rain midgame.

### vs Trick Room / Farigiraf
**Leads**: Lead Archaludon + Dragonite | Back Scizor + Pelipper

Use direct damage instead of relying on Fake Out or Bullet Punch into Armor Tail. Decide quickly whether to KO Farigiraf or absorb Trick Room and preserve Scizor for after room.

### vs Farigiraf + Mega Blastoise
**Leads**: Lead Archaludon + Dragonite | Back Pelipper + Scizor

Avoid priority-reliant lines; pressure Blastoise or Farigiraf based on preview. Pelipper supports later with rain and Wide Guard positioning.

### vs Mega Venusaur balance
**Leads**: Lead Sneasler + Dragonite | Back Archaludon + Pelipper

Choose Mega Dragonite. Do not make Basculegion the main win condition into Water-resistant Giga Drain Venusaur; use rain to enable Hurricane and reduce Fire pressure.

### vs Coil Hypnosis Milotic
**Leads**: Lead Sneasler + Archaludon | Back Scizor + Pelipper

Deny the first free Coil and keep damage on Milotic. Pelipper is optional tempo support, not an automatic lead.

### vs Fairy-heavy
**Leads**: Lead Scizor + Sneasler | Back Archaludon + Pelipper

Choose Mega Scizor and preserve it for Bullet Punch cleanup. Sneasler creates tempo while Archaludon provides special Steel pressure.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Leading Pelipper by default into Tyranitar or Mega Charizard Y weather.
- Trying to bring both Scizor and Dragonite every game despite only one Mega being usable per battle.
- Treating Basculegion as the primary win condition into Mega Venusaur or bulky Grass/Water sustain teams.
- Using banned Champions items such as Assault Vest, Life Orb, Choice Band, or Safety Goggles.
