# Jacob's Reg M-A Meta Counter Teams

*Three complete builds designed to attack the current Champions meta from different angles. All teams assume best-of-three, open team sheet.*

---

## 🎯 Meta Snapshot (as of April 19, 2026)

The Reg M-A meta is defined by three pillars:

- **Fake Out pressure** — Incineroar (near-universal) and Sneasler (47% usage) disrupt every turn 1.
- **Priority/physical offense** — Kingambit Sucker Punch, Mega Dragonite Extreme Speed, Sneasler's speed tier.
- **Redirection meta** — Sinistcha Rage Powder + Trick Room + Strength Sap is on ~39% of Incineroar teams.

**The overarching game plan for countering this:** Block their disruption, neutralize priority, and force unfavorable speed scenarios. Each team below does this differently.

> **Analogy:** The meta is a boxer who throws a fast jab (Fake Out), a power punch (Sneasler/Kingambit), and clinches when in trouble (Sinistcha redirection). These three teams each counter a different part of that combo — you're picking which weakness to exploit.

## 🧠 Team 1: Psychic Terrain Tailwind Balance ⭐

**The idea:** Terrain blocks their jab entirely. No Fake Out, no Sucker Punch, no Extreme Speed while Psychic Terrain is up. You're not just dodging — you're making half their moveset illegal.

**Core concept:** Psyspam-Tailwind hybrid. Indeedee-F sets Psychic Terrain, Whimsicott provides speed control via Tailwind, and Mega Gardevoir cleans up with Pixilate-boosted Hyper Voice.

### The Team

**1. Indeedee-F @ Psychic Seed**
- Ability: Psychic Surge
- Nature: Calm (+SpD, -Atk)
- EVs: 244 HP / 4 Def / 252 SpD / 4 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Grass *(blocks Spore from Amoonguss mirror)*
- Moves: **Follow Me / Helping Hand / Dazzling Gleam / Protect**

*Why:* Psychic Seed activates instantly from her own Terrain for +1 SpD, making her stupidly bulky on the special side. Follow Me + Helping Hand power up Gardevoir's Hyper Voice. Dazzling Gleam gives her a real attacking option vs. Dark cores.

**2. Mega Gardevoir @ Gardevoirite**
- Ability: Trace → Pixilate (post-Mega)
- Nature: Modest (+SpA, -Atk)
- EVs: 180 HP / 252 SpA / 76 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Fire *(flips the Steel matchup)*
- Moves: **Hyper Voice / Psyshock / Mystical Fire / Protect**

*Why:* Mega Gardevoir's Pixilate turns Hyper Voice into a hyper-boosted Fairy-type STAB spread move. Under Tailwind she outspeeds most of the meta. Psyshock breaks Assault Vest walls, Mystical Fire handles Steels (Kingambit, Metagross, Gholdengo).

**3. Whimsicott @ Covert Cloak**
- Ability: Prankster
- Nature: Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- EVs: 252 HP / 4 Def / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Ghost *(becomes Normal/Fighting immune)*
- Moves: **Tailwind / Encore / Moonblast / Fake Tears**

*Why:* Covert Cloak blocks Fake Out without burning a Mental Herb. Prankster Tailwind swings momentum; Encore locks setup moves (Dragon Dance, Calm Mind, Trick Room). Fake Tears is a slept-on move that doubles Gardevoir's next Hyper Voice damage.

**4. Gholdengo @ Choice Specs**
- Ability: Good as Gold
- Nature: Modest
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Fighting *(anti-Kingambit, anti-Tyranitar)*
- Moves: **Make It Rain / Shadow Ball / Dazzling Gleam / Power Gem**

*Why:* Good as Gold immunity to status is huge vs. Spore/WoW meta. Make It Rain spread damage synergizes with Hyper Voice for double spread pressure. Tera Fighting flips Kingambit matchup.

**5. Garchomp @ Life Orb**
- Ability: Rough Skin
- Nature: Jolly (+Spe, -SpA)
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Steel *(resists Ice, Fairy, Dragon — huge vs. mirror)*
- Moves: **Earthquake / Dragon Claw / Rock Slide / Protect**

*Why:* Your ground check + anti-Kingambit tool. EQ under Tailwind hits like a truck. Tera Steel flips Ice weakness.

**6. Amoonguss @ Rocky Helmet**
- Ability: Regenerator
- Nature: Sassy (+SpD, -Spe), 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 244 HP / 172 Def / 92 SpD
- Tera Type: Water
- Moves: **Spore / Rage Powder / Pollen Puff / Protect**

*Why:* Backup redirector for matchups where Indeedee can't lead. Rocky Helmet punishes physical contact (Sneasler, Kingambit). Spore is your emergency disruption tool.

### Game Plan

**Standard lead:** Indeedee-F + Mega Gardevoir
- Turn 1: Indeedee Follow Me, Gardevoir Mega Evolves + Hyper Voice. Terrain up, spread damage out, priority locked.
- Turn 2: If Whimsicott is out back, consider pivoting for Tailwind. Otherwise keep the pressure on.

**Alt lead vs. Trick Room teams:** Whimsicott + Gholdengo
- Whimsicott Encores the TR setter before they can set. Gholdengo Make It Rain hits the board.

**Win Condition:** Mega Gardevoir + Tailwind sweep once Sneasler is dead.

### Matchup Notes

| Enemy Archetype | Bring | Key Play |
|---|---|---|
| Rain (Pelipper + Archaludon) | Indeedee / Gardevoir / Garchomp / Gholdengo | EQ the Archaludon, Hyper Voice Pelipper. Tera Steel Garchomp if needed. |
| Sun (Charizard Y + Venusaur) | Indeedee / Gardevoir / Garchomp / Whimsicott | Encore Sun setter, Psyshock Venusaur. Garchomp EQ Zard through Tailwind. |
| Trick Room (Hatterene core) | Whimsicott / Gholdengo / Gardevoir / Garchomp | Prankster Taunt Hatterene turn 1. Never let TR up. |
| Snow (Mega Froslass) | Indeedee / Gardevoir / Gholdengo / Amoonguss | Spore Froslass, Gholdengo eats Blizzards (Steel resist). |

### Team Weaknesses

- **Steel spam** — Mega Metagross or Aegislash teams force you to lean hard on Mystical Fire and Tera Fire.
- **Opposing Psychic Terrain mirror** — Comes down to the Tera wars and who clicks Taunt first.

## 🥊 Team 2: Sleep + Redirection Control

**The idea:** Put someone to sleep, redirect threats, set up Mega Gyarados. Every turn, the opponent is reacting to you — they're stuck choosing who to save.

**Core concept:** Amoonguss Spore disruption + Whimsicott Prankster control + Mega Gyarados as the sledgehammer wincon.

> **Analogy:** A boxer with a strong jab, a clinch, and a haymaker. Amoonguss jabs with Spore (takes a piece off the board), Whimsicott clinches with Encore/Taunt (kills their plan), Gyarados throws the haymaker with +1 Atk Waterfall.

### The Team

**1. Amoonguss @ Covert Cloak**
- Ability: Regenerator
- Nature: Sassy (+SpD, -Spe), 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 156 Def / 100 SpD
- Tera Type: Water
- Moves: **Spore / Rage Powder / Pollen Puff / Clear Smog**

*Why:* Covert Cloak blocks Incineroar Fake Out turn 1 — a free Spore setup. Clear Smog undoes enemy setup (anti-Dragon Dance, anti-Calm Mind). Regenerator heals on switch for Gyarados setup turns.

**2. Whimsicott @ Mental Herb**
- Ability: Prankster
- Nature: Timid
- EVs: 252 HP / 4 Def / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Grass
- Moves: **Tailwind / Encore / Taunt / Moonblast**

*Why:* Mental Herb blocks one Taunt/Encore from opponents, ensuring you get your Prankster utility off. Taunt over Fake Tears on this build — you need to shut down Sinistcha Rage Powder and TR setters.

**3. Mega Gyarados @ Gyaradosite**
- Ability: Intimidate → Mold Breaker (post-Mega)
- Nature: Jolly (+Spe, -SpA)
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Flying *(boosts Waterfall? No — use Water for Waterfall STAB boost, or Dark for Crunch)*
- Tera Type: **Water**
- Moves: **Dragon Dance / Waterfall / Crunch / Protect**

*Why:* Mold Breaker post-Mega ignores Levitate (Rotom-Wash) and Multiscale (Dragonite). DD behind Amoonguss redirection is how you close games. Intimidate on entry is a free -1 Atk.

**4. Incineroar @ Safety Goggles**
- Ability: Intimidate
- Nature: Careful (+SpD, -SpA)
- EVs: 244 HP / 4 Atk / 4 Def / 252 SpD / 4 Spe
- Tera Type: Grass *(anti-Fighting)*
- Moves: **Fake Out / Parting Shot / Flare Blitz / Knock Off**

*Why:* Safety Goggles makes Incineroar immune to Spore — critical for the Amoonguss mirror. Parting Shot pivots into Gyarados setup turns safely. Knock Off over Throat Chop for item disruption (kills opposing Rocky Helmet, Sitrus Berry plans).

**5. Dragonite @ Lum Berry**
- Ability: Multiscale (NOT Mega — paired wincon)
- Nature: Adamant (+Atk, -SpA)
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Normal *(boosts Extreme Speed)*
- Moves: **Dragon Dance / Extreme Speed / Earthquake / Protect**

*Why:* Your backup wincon if Gyarados can't close. Lum Berry blocks the surprise Spore/WoW. Tera Normal Espeed is nuclear — this is a hidden wincon most teams don't prepare for.

**6. Rotom-Wash @ Sitrus Berry**
- Ability: Levitate
- Nature: Modest (+SpA, -Atk)
- EVs: 252 HP / 84 Def / 4 SpA / 164 SpD / 4 Spe
- Tera Type: Water
- Moves: **Hydro Pump / Thunderbolt / Will-O-Wisp / Protect**

*Why:* Ground immunity + Will-O-Wisp spreads burn across physical attackers (massive vs. Kingambit, Sneasler, Garchomp). Your safety valve in long games.

### Game Plan

**Standard lead:** Amoonguss + Incineroar
- Turn 1: Amoonguss Spore the biggest threat, Incineroar Fake Out the second. Free turn.
- Turn 2: Bring Whimsicott for Tailwind, or pivot Incineroar Parting Shot into Gyarados.

**DD setup sequence:** Amoonguss Rage Powder → Gyarados Dragon Dance → next turn Waterfall/Crunch double-target.

**Win Condition:** +2 Gyarados sweep OR Tera Normal Dragonite cleanup.

### Matchup Notes

| Enemy Archetype | Bring | Key Play |
|---|---|---|
| Rain cores | Amoonguss / Incineroar / Dragonite / Rotom | Spore the Archaludon, Rotom WoW the water breaker. |
| Psyspam | Whimsicott / Incineroar / Gyarados / Dragonite | Taunt Indeedee turn 1. Gyarados Crunch Gardevoir. |
| Kingambit-heavy | Whimsicott / Rotom / Gyarados / Amoonguss | WoW Kingambit early. DD Gyarados safely behind redirection. |
| Sinistcha + TR | Whimsicott / Incineroar / Gyarados / Rotom | Taunt Sinistcha. Don't let TR go up — you lose speed control. |

### Team Weaknesses

- **Electric types with Ground coverage** — Miraidon-style threats (if legal) hit Gyarados and Rotom.
- **Grass types that laugh at Spore** — Rillaboom, Amoonguss mirror. Dragonite EQ handles Rillaboom.

## 🧩 Team 3: Trick Room Bulky Offense

**The idea:** Flip the speed tier. When TR is up, your 40-speed monsters are faster than their 120-speed threats. The meta doesn't prepare for TR as well as it thinks.

**Core concept:** Hatterene Magic Bounce + Indeedee-F Psychic Surge combo. Psychic Terrain blocks Fake Out during TR setup. Heavy slow hitters clean up.

> **Analogy:** Chess where you flip the board. Their queens and bishops (fast threats) can't reach you — your pieces on the back row (slow tanks) suddenly have the initiative.

### The Team

**1. Hatterene @ Mental Herb**
- Ability: Magic Bounce
- Nature: Quiet (+SpA, -Spe), 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Water *(Fire weakness flip)*
- Moves: **Trick Room / Expanding Force / Dazzling Gleam / Protect**

*Why:* Magic Bounce auto-reflects Taunt, Spore, Will-O-Wisp, Encore back at the sender. Mental Herb is the backup vs. Prankster Taunt through items. Psychic Surge from Indeedee-F paired alongside Hatterene prevents the Fake Out disruption that normally stops Trick Room setters. Expanding Force under both Psychic Terrain + TR is a nuclear spread move.

**2. Indeedee-F @ Sitrus Berry**
- Ability: Psychic Surge
- Nature: Relaxed (+Def, -Spe), 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Grass *(anti-Rage Powder effects don't matter here; Grass resists Ground EQ spread)*
- Moves: **Follow Me / Trick Room / Helping Hand / Dazzling Gleam**

*Why:* Second TR setter for redundancy. Follow Me protects Hatterene's setup turn. Helping Hand doubles Ursaluna's Facade damage in TR.

**3. Ursaluna @ Flame Orb**
- Ability: Guts
- Nature: Brave (+Atk, -Spe), 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Ghost *(immune to Fighting/Normal — huge vs. Sneasler/Kingambit)*
- Moves: **Facade / Headlong Rush / Earthquake / Protect**

*Why:* Guts + Burn = 2.25x Attack on Facade (also doubled by burn effect removal). Under TR at 52 base Speed, Ursaluna moves first against almost everything. Tera Ghost flips the Ground immunity on Headlong Rush teammates.

**4. Torkoal @ Charcoal**
- Ability: Drought
- Nature: Quiet, 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Fire *(double Fire STAB under Sun)*
- Moves: **Eruption / Heat Wave / Earth Power / Protect**

*Why:* Drought sun + TR + full HP Eruption = game-ending damage. Earth Power for Heatran/Steel types. Under TR, Torkoal is effectively "fast" and clicks Eruption before damage.

**5. Incineroar @ Safety Goggles**
- Ability: Intimidate
- Nature: Careful
- EVs: 244 HP / 4 Atk / 4 Def / 252 SpD / 4 Spe
- Tera Type: Grass
- Moves: **Fake Out / Parting Shot / Flare Blitz / Throat Chop**

*Why:* Incineroar works on TR teams as a pivot. Parting Shot into Hatterene/Torkoal is how you safely setup. Safety Goggles vs. Spore threats.

**6. Rhyperior @ Weakness Policy**
- Ability: Solid Rock
- Nature: Brave, 0 Spe IVs
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Ground *(boosts EQ STAB)*
- Moves: **Rock Slide / Earthquake / High Horsepower / Protect**

*Why:* Solid Rock reduces SE damage by 25% — makes WP activation near-guaranteed but survivable. Rhyperior's Earthquake plus Rock Slide hits everything at base 40 Speed under Trick Room. High Horsepower is the single-target EQ that doesn't hit your own mons.

### Game Plan

**Standard lead:** Hatterene + Indeedee-F
- Turn 1: Psychic Terrain + Trick Room up in one turn. Follow Me if opponent threatens.
- Turn 2: Pivot in Ursaluna or Torkoal. Clean up.

**Alt lead vs. Taunt/Encore:** Incineroar + Indeedee-F, Parting Shot into Hatterene next turn.

**Win Condition:** Ursaluna 2HKOs the field under Helping Hand + TR, OR Torkoal Eruption nuke.

### Matchup Notes

| Enemy Archetype | Bring | Key Play |
|---|---|---|
| Tailwind teams | Hatterene / Indeedee / Ursaluna / Rhyperior | Race to TR — their Tailwind + your TR cancel, you win on slow attackers. |
| Rain (Pelipper) | Hatterene / Indeedee / Torkoal / Ursaluna | Torkoal's Drought overrides Rain on switch. Eruption nuke. |
| Sun mirror | Incineroar / Hatterene / Ursaluna / Rhyperior | Intimidate + Protect stall Charizard Y. Set TR turn 2. |
| Priority-heavy (Kingambit/Dnite) | Hatterene / Indeedee / Ursaluna / Rhyperior | Psychic Terrain neuters Sucker Punch/Espeed. |

### Team Weaknesses

- **Fast Taunt before TR** — Prankster Whimsicott gets reflected by Magic Bounce (good), but non-Prankster fast Taunters (like Dragapult with Taunt) can threaten Indeedee.
- **Opposing Trick Room** — Both TRs cancel, neutral matchup. Hope your slow hitters outscale.
- **Wide Guard** — Blocks Rock Slide, Eruption, Heat Wave. Bring High Horsepower/Facade as single-target backup.

## 🎯 My Recommendation for Build Order

1. **Build Team 3 (Trick Room) first** — easiest to pilot, highest floor, immediate meta counter to your own weather-aggro teams, and fastest to tournament-ready.
2. **Then Team 1 (Psyspam Tailwind)** — highest ceiling, rewards your existing pivoting instincts from Rain A/B/C.
3. **Team 2 (Sleep + Disruption)** is your "flex" team for longer tournaments — save it for top cut when opponents have scouted your other two.
