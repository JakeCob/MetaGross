### 🚨 Fake Out Nerf — changes the whole format

**Fake Out now works ONCE per Pokemon per battle.** Switching Incineroar out and back in does NOT refresh it. So:

- Incineroar — 1 Fake Out per game
- Sneasler — 1 Fake Out per game
- Mega Kangaskhan — 1 Fake Out per game (if it runs it)

**Implication:** you can't chain Fake Out disruption like past VGC formats. Spend it at the highest-value turn of the match.

## 🧪 Fake Out Decision Tree

With one Fake Out per Pokemon per game:

**Click Fake Out when**
- Breaking an opposing Focus Sash (guaranteeing a KO that turn)
- Stopping a setup move (Dragon Dance, Calm Mind, Trick Room)
- Preventing Wide Guard / Follow Me from shutting down next turn
- Denying a Spore turn (Amoonguss etc.)

**Do NOT click Fake Out when**
- Opponent is already locked into a move you outspeed
- The flinch doesn't prevent a critical KO
- You're about to switch out — you'd waste your single use

**Pro tip:** if the team runs both Incineroar AND Sneasler, save Sneasler's Fake Out for late-game setup turns (it pairs with Coaching for multi-turn plays).
