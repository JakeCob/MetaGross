## 🏆 Wolfe Glick's #1 Ranked Team (Team 6)

### Context

On April 13, 2026, Wolfe Glick (2016 World Champion, 10-time Regional Champion) announced he hit **#1 ranked player in the world in Pokémon Champions** with Mega Scovillain. He closed out the run on an 11-win streak. The team has since been studied and documented by the wider community.

> **Why this matters:** Wolfe is widely considered one of the most influential team builders in VGC history. His picks often define the next meta. When Wolfe Mega Evolves a Scovillain, the community pays attention.

### The Core Insight — Spicy Spray

Most players look at Mega Scovillain and see a bulky Grass/Fire attacker. Wolfe saw something else: **Spicy Spray is the anti-meta ability.**

When a Pokémon makes direct contact with Mega Scovillain, it gets **burned**. No accuracy check, no save, just burned.

Now think about what the current meta uses:
- **Sneasler** Close Combat — contact ✅ gets burned
- **Kingambit** Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave — contact ✅ gets burned
- **Incineroar** Fake Out / Flare Blitz / Knock Off — contact ✅ gets burned
- **Basculegion** Wave Crash — contact ✅ gets burned
- **Mega Dragonite** Extreme Speed — contact ✅ gets burned

> **Analogy:** Imagine a boxer who you can't punch without your glove catching fire. Every physical attacker in the meta has to think twice about contacting Scovillain — and burn cuts their Attack in half permanently.

### The Team

**1. Mega Scovillain @ Scovillainite**
- Ability: Chlorophyll → **Spicy Spray** (post-Mega)
- Nature: Calm (+SpD, -Atk)
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 SpD / 4 SpA (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Water *(fixes Fire & Rock weaknesses)*
- Moves: **Flamethrower / Leech Seed / Rage Powder / Protect**

*The engine. Rage Powder redirects physical hits into the Spicy Spray trap. Leech Seed is the chip engine. Flamethrower is the STAB damage option.*

**2. Sneasler @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Unburden
- Nature: Adamant
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Fighting
- Moves: **Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / Coaching**

*The key innovation: **Coaching on Sneasler.** Coaching boosts Attack AND Defense by +1 for the target. Sneasler Coaches Primarina or Kingambit for a massive setup turn.*

**3. Primarina @ Leftovers**
- Ability: **Liquid Voice**
- Nature: Modest
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: Water or Steel
- Moves: **Hyper Voice / Moonblast / Calm Mind / Protect**

*Liquid Voice converts Hyper Voice from Normal-type → Water-type. With STAB + Calm Mind, Primarina becomes a special sweeper that gets stronger every turn behind Scovillain's redirection. Tera Water makes Hyper Voice 2x STAB. And because Hyper Voice isn't a contact move, it bypasses Spicy Spray — so Primarina can safely attack while Scovillain redirects.*

**4. Kingambit @ Black Glasses**
- Ability: **Defiant**
- Nature: Adamant
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD
- Tera Type: Flying or Fire (anti-Fighting)
- Moves: **Kowtow Cleave / Sucker Punch / Swords Dance / Protect**

*Defiant over Supreme Overlord — when opposing Incineroar Intimidates, Kingambit gets +2 Atk instead of -1. Combined with Coaching from Sneasler, you can hit +3 Atk on turn 1.*

**5. Aerodactyl @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Unnerve
- Nature: Jolly
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Flying or Ground
- Moves: **Rock Slide / Wide Guard / Tailwind / Protect**

*The speed control + anti-spread tech. Wide Guard blocks Rock Slide, Eruption, Muddy Water, Make It Rain — all the spread moves. Tailwind as backup to Sneasler speed mode.*

**6. Garchomp @ Haban Berry**
- Ability: Rough Skin
- Nature: Jolly
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera Type: Steel or Fire
- Moves: **Dragon Claw / Earthquake / Stomping Tantrum / Protect**

*Haban Berry is the tech — halves damage from a super-effective Dragon move. Specifically saves vs. opposing Mega Dragonite / Dragapult Draco Meteor. Stomping Tantrum for dealing with opposing Sashers safely (doubles if the previous move failed).*

### The Game Plan

**Standard lead:** Scovillain + Primarina
- Turn 1: Scovillain Rage Powder, Primarina Calm Mind.
- Opponent hits Scovillain with a physical move → burned → -50% Atk permanently.
- Turn 2: Scovillain Leech Seed, Primarina Hyper Voice (Water STAB at +1 SpA) hits both sides.
- Turn 3: Opponent's physical attacker is now at half Atk AND burning AND leech seeded. Primarina cleans up.

**Alt lead:** Sneasler + Aerodactyl
- Turn 1: Sneasler Fake Out the biggest threat, Aerodactyl Tailwind.
- Turn 2: Pivot Sneasler out, bring Scovillain for the redirection wall.

**Late-game wincon:** Sneasler Coaching → Kingambit Swords Dance → +3 Atk Kowtow Cleave sweep.

### Lead Options (from the official team report)

- Scovillain + Primarina (standard)
- Sneasler + Kingambit (aggressive)
- Aerodactyl + Garchomp (speed control + physical pressure)

### Key Threats to This Team

- **Fast special attackers** that don't make contact (can't be burned): Mega Floette Light of Ruin, Mega Alakazam Psychic
- **Spread move users** when Aerodactyl isn't positioned right (Wide Guard must be up)
- **Tyranitar Sand** — wears down Scovillain since it's not Rock/Ground/Steel

### Why It Worked for Wolfe

The team exploits a meta blind spot. Most builders are designing offense around speed tiers and Fake Out chains. Spicy Spray punishes the entire physical meta with one passive ability. It's a **counterteam disguised as a balance team.**

### 🚨 Critical Thresholds & Tera Call-Outs

1. **Mega Floette must Tera Poison (not Fire) vs. Sneasler.** Post-Tera Poison: Fighting hits 0.5x. Fire doesn't resist Fighting.
2. **Kingambit must Tera Flying vs. Ground/Fighting.** Currently 4x weak to both; Tera Flying makes you immune to Ground and 0.5x to Fighting.
3. **Zoroark Tera Fighting is the offensive option** — converts you to pure Fighting for STAB Close Combat... wait, Zoroark doesn't learn Close Combat. Better: **Tera Ghost** to double down on Bitter Malice STAB, or **Tera Fire** to nuke Sinistcha/Steel.
