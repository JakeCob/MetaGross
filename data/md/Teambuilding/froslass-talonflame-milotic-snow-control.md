## Game Plan

Use Talonflame Tailwind or Froslass pressure to take early tempo, then rotate Rotom-Wash and Milotic to punish physical balance and Intimidate lines. Sneasler handles Kingambit/Tyranitar/Steel threats, while Scarf Garchomp gives immediate speed and spread pressure.

## Win Conditions
- Froslass snow-boosted Blizzard pressure plus Shadow Ball/Thunderbolt coverage
- Milotic Competitive punishing Incineroar or Arcanine-Hisui positioning
- Scarf Garchomp cleaning with Earthquake/Rock Slide after Talonflame Tailwind or Icy Wind support
- Sneasler forcing early trades with Fake Out + Close Combat
