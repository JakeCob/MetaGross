## 🎯 Full Team Build (Exact Wolfe Version)

### 1. Mega Scovillain @ Scovillainite — The Engine
- **Ability:** Chlorophyll → Spicy Spray (post-Mega)
- **Nature:** Calm (+SpD, -Atk)
- **EVs:** 252 HP / 196 Def / 60 SpD (IVs: 0 Atk, 0 Spe)
- **Tera Type:** Water *(resolves Fire + Rock + Ice weaknesses in one)*
- **Moves:** Flamethrower / Leech Seed / Rage Powder / Protect

**Why these EVs:** Max HP + mixed defenses so Scovillain survives BOTH physical spread moves (Rock Slide, Earthquake) AND special pressure (Moonblast, Hydro Pump). 0 Spe IVs so Scovillain acts last, meaning you see opponent's move choice before Rage Powder commits.

### 2. Sneasler @ Focus Sash — The Coach
- **Ability:** Unburden
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpA)
- **EVs:** 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- **Tera Type:** Fighting *(boosts Close Combat for OHKO thresholds on Kingambit)*
- **Moves:** Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / **Coaching**

**The key innovation:** Coaching boosts Attack AND Defense +1 for an ally. Turn 1: Fake Out the biggest threat. Turn 2: Coaching → Kingambit or Primarina. Game over.

### 3. Primarina @ Leftovers — The Sweeper
- **Ability:** Liquid Voice
- **Nature:** Modest (+SpA, -Atk)
- **EVs:** 244 HP / 4 Def / 252 SpA / 4 SpD / 4 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- **Tera Type:** Water *(2x STAB on Liquid-Voiced Hyper Voice)* or Steel *(Fairy resist bulk)*
- **Moves:** Hyper Voice / Moonblast / Calm Mind / Protect

**The sound trick:** Liquid Voice turns Hyper Voice into a Water-type spread move. Because it's a sound move, it bypasses Substitute AND isn't a contact move — so Primarina can attack freely without triggering Spicy Spray on enemies while your Scovillain Rage Powders.

### 4. Kingambit @ Black Glasses — The Win Condition
- **Ability:** Defiant
- **Nature:** Adamant
- **EVs:** 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD
- **Tera Type:** Flying *(flips 4x Ground/Fighting weakness to immunity/resist)*
- **Moves:** Kowtow Cleave / Sucker Punch / Swords Dance / Protect

**Defiant over Supreme Overlord:** When opposing Incineroar switches in with Intimidate, Kingambit gets +2 Atk instead of -1. Net swing: +3 effective Attack. Combined with Coaching from Sneasler, you're sitting at +3/+4 on turn 2.

### 5. Aerodactyl @ Focus Sash — The Pivot
- **Ability:** Unnerve
- **Nature:** Jolly (+Spe, -SpA)
- **EVs:** 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- **Tera Type:** Flying or Ground *(choose based on opposing lead)*
- **Moves:** Rock Slide / **Wide Guard** / Tailwind / Protect

**Wide Guard is your anti-Eruption, anti-Make It Rain, anti-Muddy Water button.** Aerodactyl's 130 base Speed beats Whimsicott (116) — you win the Tailwind race against most opposing setters.

### 6. Garchomp @ Haban Berry — The Breaker
- **Ability:** Rough Skin
- **Nature:** Jolly
- **EVs:** 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- **Tera Type:** Steel *(flip Ice 4x weakness; survive Basculegion Ice Fang)* or Fire *(anti-Mega Scizor mirror)*
- **Moves:** Dragon Claw / Earthquake / **Stomping Tantrum** / Protect

**Haban Berry tech:** Halves damage from a super-effective Dragon move. Lets Garchomp survive a surprise Mega Dragonite Draco Meteor or Dragapult Dragon Darts and threaten back. Stomping Tantrum doubles to 150 BP after a missed/failed previous move — nasty follow-up when opposing Focus Sash mon survives turn 1.

## 🎮 Turn-by-Turn Playbook vs. Each Archetype

### vs. Rain + Tailwind (Pelipper / Basculegion / Archaludon / Dragonite)

**Lead:** Aerodactyl + Garchomp
- **Turn 1:** Aerodactyl Tailwind, Garchomp Earthquake (hits Archaludon 2HKO, doesn't hit Pelipper/Flying). Pelipper likely Tailwind back.
- **Turn 2:** Pivot Aerodactyl → Scovillain. Garchomp clicks Stomping Tantrum on Basculegion switch-in.
- **Turn 3:** Scovillain Rage Powder, Primarina switch-in Calm Mind.
- **Wincon:** +2 Primarina Hyper Voice OHKOs Pelipper and Basculegion in the rain (Water STAB stacks with Rain).

**Key insight:** Rain BOOSTS your Primarina's Liquid Voice Hyper Voice. Your opponent's rain fuels your sweeper.

### vs. Sun (Charizard Y / Venusaur / Incineroar / Aegislash)

**Lead:** Aerodactyl + Kingambit
- **Turn 1:** Aerodactyl Rock Slide (OHKOs Charizard Y through Sash at 185%+). Kingambit Kowtow Cleave Venusaur (2HKO, Dark vs Grass).
- **Turn 2:** If Venusaur Sleep Powders, Kingambit was probably asleep. Aerodactyl Tailwind.
- **Turn 3:** Bring Scovillain to wall. Spicy Spray any Fighting/Dark attacker.

**Key insight:** Sun WEAKENS your Liquid Voice Hyper Voice (water in sun = 0.5x). Don't lead Primarina vs. Sun.

### vs. Trick Room (Hatterene / Indeedee-F / Ursaluna / Torkoal)

**Lead:** Sneasler + Aerodactyl
- **Turn 1:** Sneasler Fake Out the Hatterene (break Mental Herb or force the switch). Aerodactyl Tailwind.
- **Turn 2:** Sneasler Dire Claw Hatterene (3HKO but 30% status = potentially sleeps the TR setter). Aerodactyl Rock Slide both.
- **Turn 3:** If TR goes up anyway, Scovillain + Primarina behind Rage Powder. Spicy Spray punishes Ursaluna Facade contact.

**Key insight:** Ursaluna is a problem (Guts ignores burn). Lead with speed control to prevent TR setup.

### vs. Mega Floette Goodstuffs (Floette / Incineroar / Sneasler / Kingambit / Aerodactyl)

**Lead:** Sneasler + Kingambit (mirror match)
- **Turn 1:** Sneasler Fake Out Floette (breaks Focus Sash or blocks Calm Mind turn). Kingambit Kowtow Cleave Floette (OHKO).
- **Turn 2:** Opposing Sneasler vs. your Sneasler. Coaching your Kingambit → +1 Def / +1 Atk.
- **Turn 3:** Kingambit Swords Dance behind Scovillain Rage Powder. Now at +3. Sweep.

**Key insight:** Mirror depends on who Coaches first. Your Kingambit + Coaching is faster to setup than opponent's Calm Mind Floette.

### vs. Snow (Mega Froslass / Milotic / Incineroar / Garchomp / Sneasler / Sinistcha — Jacob's own team!)

**Lead:** Aerodactyl + Scovillain
- **Turn 1:** Aerodactyl Wide Guard (blocks Blizzard from Froslass + Icy Wind from Milotic). Scovillain Flamethrower Froslass (2HKO through Snow).
- **Turn 2:** Scovillain Rage Powder, Aerodactyl Tailwind. Opposing Milotic tries Hypnosis → Scovillain (Grass-type → can't sleep in sun? No that's Spore only. Hypnosis hits). But Aerodactyl is airborne.
- **Turn 3:** Bring Primarina. +1 Calm Mind. Sweep through the Snow (Water neutral in Snow).

**Key insight:** Snow doesn't interact with your team's damage. Fire through the ice.

### vs. Psyspam / Balance (Gardevoir / Primarina / Whimsicott / Sneasler / Arcanine / Kingambit — QueerCrocodile's winning team)

**Lead:** Aerodactyl + Sneasler
- **Turn 1:** Sneasler Fake Out Whimsicott (prevents Tailwind turn 1). Aerodactyl Rock Slide (hits both).
- **Turn 2:** Aerodactyl Tailwind, Sneasler Dire Claw Gardevoir.
- **Turn 3:** Pivot to Scovillain + Primarina. Primarina Hyper Voice out-damages opposing Primarina (Liquid Voice mirror; higher Calm Mind count wins).

**Key insight:** Prevent their Tailwind, force a speed tie race with yours. Your Rock Slide flinch hax favors you.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

1. **Mega Evolving too early.** Only Mega Evolve Scovillain when you need Spicy Spray THAT turn. Base Chlorophyll is useless without Sun — Scovillain is worse until it Megas.

2. **Protecting into Rage Powder.** When you Protect Scovillain AND Rage Powder with the same mon on the field, opponents can just target the other mon with spread. Rage Powder the turn you need it — don't double up defense.

3. **Clicking Flamethrower in Rain.** Flamethrower is weakened 50% in Rain. Use Leech Seed or switch into Primarina instead.

4. **Forgetting to Coaching before Swords Dance.** Coaching FIRST gives +1 Atk/+1 Def, Swords Dance FIRST caps Atk at +2. Doing both in the right order = +3/+1. Wrong order = +2.

5. **Tera-ing reactively instead of proactively.** Mega Scovillain's Tera Water is a planned "I saw Mega Floette in team preview, I'm saving Tera for her Light of Ruin" decision — don't save it for a situation that might not come.

## 🔧 Jacob's Custom Variant — "J-Glick" Scovillain

*A Jacob-specific variant that keeps Wolfe's core but swaps one piece for a Pokémon you already have tuned.*

### The Swap: Aerodactyl → Incineroar

Your memory: you run Incineroar on 4/5 of your existing teams. You know its EVs, its Fake Out timing, its Parting Shot pivots cold. **Aerodactyl is a new mon to learn from scratch.**

The tradeoff:
- **Lose:** Tailwind setter, Wide Guard, Rock Slide flinch pressure
- **Gain:** Intimidate (stacks with Scovillain burns for brutal Attack-cutting), Parting Shot (pivots into Primarina Calm Mind free), Fake Out (second Fake Out on the team)

### Jacob's Team

1. **Mega Scovillain** @ Scovillainite — same set
2. **Sneasler** @ Focus Sash — same set
3. **Primarina** @ Leftovers — same set
4. **Kingambit** @ Black Glasses — same set
5. **Incineroar** @ Safety Goggles — Intimidate — Fake Out / Parting Shot / Flare Blitz / Knock Off (the spread from your Rain A)
6. **Garchomp** @ Haban Berry — same set

### New Game Plan (Incineroar-centric)

**Default Lead:** Incineroar + Scovillain
- **Turn 1:** Incineroar Fake Out the biggest physical threat (they expected Scovillain to Rage Powder — surprise, no redirection needed). Scovillain Leech Seed the other target.
- **Turn 2:** Incineroar Parting Shot → Primarina. Scovillain Rage Powder.
- **Turn 3:** Primarina Calm Mind behind Rage Powder. +1 Primarina + unburned opposing physical attacker + leech seed chip. Sweep primed.

### Trade-offs to Watch

- **No Tailwind support.** Against fast offense (Dragapult, Weavile), you need to win the speed race another way. Consider swapping Garchomp → Mega Dragonite for Multiscale bulk + Extreme Speed priority as backup.
- **Less spread defense.** Without Wide Guard, enemy Rock Slide / Muddy Water hurts. Protect timing becomes critical.

### When to Use Variant vs. Pure Wolfe

- **Ranked ladder grinding:** Use variant. Incineroar's flexibility matches your instincts.
- **Tournament Bo3 with scouting:** Use pure Wolfe. The Aerodactyl Wide Guard answers spread teams your Incineroar variant loses to.
