## 🎭 Tech Module 1: Hisuian Zoroark Deception

**Your Talonflame/Zoroark encounter was the perfect example of this archetype working.** Here's the full breakdown.

### Why It Works

Hisuian Zoroark is Normal/Ghost with the **Illusion** ability — it enters battle disguised as the last Pokémon in your party. When you Fake Out'd the "Talonflame," you didn't realize:

- It was actually Zoroark looking like Talonflame
- Zoroark is **Ghost-type**, which is **immune to Normal-type Fake Out**
- You burned your Fake Out turn for nothing
- Meanwhile the real Talonflame (likely in the back) got free Tailwind setup

> **Analogy:** This is like a bluffer in poker wearing mirrored sunglasses — you're reading body language from a reflection.

### Usage Data (from Pikalytics & Pokémon Zone)

- **Ability:** Illusion (100% usage)
- **Item:** Focus Sash (51%), Choice Scarf (20%), Silk Scarf (14%)
- **Top Moves:** Icy Wind (71%), Bitter Malice (65%), Hyper Voice (57%), Protect (57%)
- **Best Disguise Partners:** Kingambit (baits Fighting moves, Zoroark immune), Mega Lopunny, Sneasler, Whimsicott
- **66 tournament appearances in Reg M-A**

### The Standard Set

**Hisuian Zoroark @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Illusion
- Nature: Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- EVs: 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: **Fighting** (becomes pure Fighting on Tera — turns Rock/Dark resists into neutral, flips Kingambit matchup)
- Moves: **Bitter Malice / Hyper Voice / Icy Wind / Protect**

**The disguise trick:** Put Kingambit in your last party slot. Zoroark enters looking like Kingambit. Opponents either:
- Use Fighting moves at "Kingambit" → Zoroark is immune (Ghost)
- Use Fake Out → Zoroark is immune (Ghost resists Normal)
- Use Sucker Punch expecting Kingambit → wastes the turn

Either way, you get a free turn of Bitter Malice or Icy Wind.

### How to Play Against It (since you've now been burned once)

- **Read team preview carefully.** If a Pokémon looks slightly "off" — e.g., a Talonflame with uncommon teammates, or a Kingambit on a speed team — suspect Zoroark.
- **Never Fake Out blindly in turn 1.** Watch for a hit that "shouldn't" work (Normal-type on a Ghost = immune).
- **Use Foresight/Odor Sleuth effects.** Not in Reg M-A meaningfully. Easier: just use STAB moves that can't miss targeting (Dazzling Gleam, Moonblast) — they'll reveal the illusion by hitting.
- **Target with spread moves.** Rock Slide, Heat Wave, Make It Rain — they hit both targets so the Zoroark takes at least a grazing hit and reveals.

## 💧 Tech Module 2: Milotic Coil + Hypnosis

This is **actually tournament-legal and working right now.** Here's why it's nasty.

### The Trick

**Hypnosis** has 60% base accuracy. Most players won't click it because it misses 4/10 times.

**Coil** (Milotic has it) boosts Attack +1, Defense +1, AND **Accuracy +1**. After one Coil, Hypnosis goes from 60% → 78%. After two Coils, 96%. Now it basically never misses.

Milotic's typical ability is **Competitive** (+2 SpA on stat drops) or **Marvel Scale** (+50% Defense when statused) — both punish the opponent for playing normally.

> **Analogy:** This is like a boxer who doesn't just hit harder each round — they start dodging better and better until your punches can't land. Then they put you to sleep.

### Usage Data

- **Ability:** Competitive, Marvel Scale, or Cute Charm
- **Top Items:** Leftovers, Sitrus Berry, Mystic Water, Lum Berry, Wacan Berry
- **Top Moves:** Scald, Protect, Icy Wind, Ice Beam, Recover, Life Dew, Muddy Water, **Hypnosis**, **Coil**, Helping Hand

### The Standard Set

**Milotic @ Leftovers**
- Ability: Competitive
- Nature: Bold (+Def, -Atk)
- EVs: 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpA (IVs: 0 Atk)
- Tera Type: **Water** (STAB Scald boost) or **Fairy** (anti-Dragon)
- Moves: **Coil / Hypnosis / Scald / Recover**

### Where It Wins

I found **multiple tournament teams using Milotic in top cuts:**

## 🐍 Team 4 (NEW): Hisuian Zoroark Deception Balance

*Inspired by the meta trend of illusion + Tailwind offense. 66 Reg M-A tournament appearances on Zoroark alone.*

**The concept:** Opponents see "Kingambit + Incineroar + Sneasler + Aerodactyl + Mega Floette + Whimsicott" in team preview — looks like Goodstuffs. But your Kingambit slot IS Zoroark. Every turn 1, they're guessing wrong.

### The Team

**1. Hisuian Zoroark @ Focus Sash** *(disguised as Kingambit)*
- Ability: Illusion
- Timid, 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe, 0 Atk IVs
- Tera: Fighting
- Moves: **Bitter Malice / Hyper Voice / Icy Wind / Protect**

**2. Aerodactyl @ Focus Sash**
- Ability: Unnerve
- Jolly, 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera: Flying (boosts Tailwind team speed; Flying STAB)
- Moves: **Tailwind / Rock Slide / Wide Guard / Protect**

*Tailwind setter + Wide Guard vs. enemy Rock Slides/Earthquakes. Aerodactyl's 130 base Speed means it often sets Tailwind before opposing Whimsicott.*

**3. Mega Floette @ Floettite**
- Ability: Flower Veil → Fairy Aura (post-Mega)
- Modest, 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD, 0 Atk IVs
- Tera: Fire (anti-Steel, anti-Ice)
- Moves: **Light of Ruin / Calm Mind / Moonblast / Protect**

*Your wincon. Fairy Aura boosts Fairy damage. One Calm Mind + Light of Ruin from Mega Floette can OHKO most of the meta.*

**4. Incineroar @ Safety Goggles**
- Ability: Intimidate
- Careful, 244 HP / 4 Atk / 4 Def / 252 SpD / 4 Spe
- Tera: Grass
- Moves: **Fake Out / Parting Shot / Flare Blitz / Knock Off**

**5. Sneasler @ White Herb**
- Ability: Unburden
- Jolly, 4 HP / 252 Atk / 252 Spe
- Tera: Fighting (Close Combat boost)
- Moves: **Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / Protect**

*After Close Combat, White Herb restores Def/SpD, then Unburden doubles Speed. Nasty under Tailwind.*

**6. Kingambit @ Assault Vest** *(in last slot — the decoy for Zoroark)*
- Ability: Supreme Overlord
- Adamant, 220 HP / 252 Atk / 36 Spe
- Tera: Flying (anti-Fighting)
- Moves: **Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave / Iron Head / Low Kick**

*MUST be in last slot. Zoroark's Illusion copies the last Pokémon's appearance. If Kingambit is slot 6, Zoroark looks like Kingambit on entry.*

### The Game Plan

**Opening lead:** Hisuian Zoroark (looking like Kingambit) + Aerodactyl
- Turn 1: Aerodactyl Tailwind, "Kingambit" (actually Zoroark) Bitter Malice/Icy Wind
- Opponent sees "Kingambit" and likely Fakes Out it (they expect Sucker Punch). Zoroark is Ghost-type — Fake Out misses. Free turn.
- Or they target "Kingambit" with Fighting coverage (expecting to break it) — Zoroark is immune. Free turn.

**Wincon:** Once illusion breaks, pivot to Mega Floette + Calm Mind sweep under Tailwind.

### Bring Order vs. Meta

| Opponent | Bring | Key Play |
|---|---|---|
| Rain teams | Zoroark / Aerodactyl / Floette / Kingambit | Icy Wind locks their Pelipper/Basculegion speed. Light of Ruin nukes Basculegion. |
| Goodstuffs (Incin/Sneasler/Kingambit) | Zoroark / Aerodactyl / Incineroar / Floette | Disguise + Tailwind + Calm Mind Floette overwhelms their pivots. |
| Trick Room | Aerodactyl / Incineroar / Sneasler / Kingambit | Leave Zoroark in back — illusion wasted if TR flips speed. |
| Sun | Zoroark / Floette / Incineroar / Sneasler | Zoroark Bitter Malice punishes Solar Power Charizard Y. |

### Team Weakness

Once the illusion breaks, Zoroark dies easily (Focus Sash saves one hit). The deception is a one-time advantage per game.

## 🔧 Refinements to Teams 1-3

Given what the tournament data shows, here are targeted upgrades:

### Team 1 (Psyspam Tailwind) Refinements
- **Swap Whimsicott → Aerodactyl** as primary Tailwind setter. Aerodactyl's 130 Speed beats Whimsicott's 116, and Wide Guard protects from Rock Slide spam.
- Keep Whimsicott as a flex slot for Encore utility vs. setup teams.
- **Replace Garchomp → Mega Floette** if you want a bigger win condition. Garchomp stays if you need Ground coverage.

### Team 2 (Sleep + Disruption) Refinements
- **Add Clefable** instead of Amoonguss for the Magic Guard immunity (hazards, burn, LO recoil). Clefable Follow Me + Helping Hand supports DD Gyarados cleanly.
- Amoonguss stays as a sideboard option for heavy status-susceptible teams.

### Team 3 (Trick Room Offense) Refinements
- **Consider Farigiraf over Indeedee-F.** Armor Tail blocks ALL priority moves targeting your team — stops Kingambit Sucker Punch, Mega Dragonite Extreme Speed, AND Fake Out. Indeedee-F still wins on raw Psychic Surge synergy with Expanding Force.
- **Add Maushold as flex** for Population Bomb wincon under TR. Technician + Wide Lens = 10 hits at boosted damage.
